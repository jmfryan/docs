Intercom's security program, compliance certifications, and live security posture are published on our **Trust Center** at [trust.intercom.com](https://trust.intercom.com/).

The Trust Center is the canonical, always-up-to-date source for everything related to Intercom's security. Information lives there rather than in this documentation site so that compliance reports, sub-processor lists, and certifications stay fresh as soon as they change — without waiting on a docs deploy.

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in Intercom, please report it through the Trust Center rather than filing a public issue. Our security team monitors submissions there and will respond promptly.

<Note>
  For anything urgent or customer-impacting, contact Intercom support directly in addition to filing through the Trust Center.
</Note>
